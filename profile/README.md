## Overview
DJI Pilot 2 for Windows brings the camera view, flight data, and aircraft controls into one screen, so pilots can watch a flight and adjust settings without juggling separate tools. Most pilots rely on the DJI Pilot 2 app to keep video, telemetry, and mission planning in one place instead of switching between tools mid-flight.

## Features
**Live camera feed.** Watch the aircraft's video feed in real time while flying. **Flight telemetry.** Keep altitude, speed, battery, and signal strength visible throughout the flight. **Mission planning.** Lay out waypoints and routes ahead of a flight. **Camera and gimbal controls.** Adjust camera settings and gimbal position from the same screen.
